Moon Information

The Moon

Earth's moon is the only natural satellite of Earth. It reflects sunlight and orbits our planet

  • Type: Natural Satellite
  • Orbits: Earth
  • Atmosphere: Extremely thin exosphere

About the Planet

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the sun. It has liquid water on its surface and supports a wide variety of life.

  • Position: Third planet from the sun
  • Natural Satellites: One
  • Planet Type: Terrestial planet

Explore Other Moons

Mars

Mars has two natural moons: Phobos and Deimos.

Phobos

Phobos is the larger and closer of mar's two moons.

Deimos

Deimos is the smaller and more distant moon of mars

Jupiter

Jupiter has many moons, including four well-known moons: Io, Europa, Ganymede, and callisto.

Saturn

Saturn has many moons, including Titan and Enceladus. Titan is Saturn's largest moon

Uranus

Uranus has many moons, including Titania, Oberon, Miranda, Ariel, and Umbriel.

Pluto

Pluto is a dwarf planet with five known moons. It's largest moon is Charon.
